FAQ

Fair questions.

Straight answers — the way the rest of this works.

How soon can I be seen?

Usually within one to two weeks — not months. Book online and pick the time that works for you, including evenings.

Is telehealth effective for an ADHD assessment?

Yes. The screening is computer-based and designed for remote completion, and the clinical interview works fully over secure video. Many clients prefer it — no driving, no waiting rooms.

What if it turns out not to be ADHD?

Then you'll know that, too — with an explanation of what the results do suggest and where to go next. Clarity either way is the point.

Why cash pay instead of insurance?

Three reasons: no surprise bills, no one telling us how long your visit can be, and privacy — nothing about ADHD goes on your insurance record. Many clients use HSA/FSA funds.

Do you prescribe medication?

When clinically appropriate, yes — including starting, adjusting, or changing medications as part of your treatment plan.

What ages do you see?

Adolescents through adults, via telehealth anywhere in Kentucky.
