## 1. How does an adult ADHD assessment work?

An adult ADHD assessment at Blue Willow starts with an at-home screening, followed by a 60–90 minute video visit. The provider reviews your history and screening, asks questions, and discusses next steps with you. The assessment explains what the information supports; it does not begin with a presumed answer.

### What to expect

You book through the secure portal and complete the screening from home before the appointment. During the video visit, you can talk through your questions, relevant history, and the screening results. The provider uses the full conversation and available information to guide the assessment and discuss a plan. You can attend by telehealth from anywhere in Kentucky.

### What this page can't tell you

The process is the same from one appointment to the next, but what it finds is personal. A description of the steps cannot determine whether ADHD explains your experience. That takes an individual assessment.

## 6. What's the difference between an online ADHD quiz and a real assessment?

An online quiz can offer screening questions, but a quiz by itself cannot provide an individual assessment. Blue Willow's process combines an at-home screening with a 60–90 minute provider review, including a conversation about your history and questions. The screening is one part of the assessment, not the whole process.

### Screening and assessment serve different purposes

A quiz is completed on its own and may give you a result to think about. An assessment adds a provider's review of the screening and your personal context. During the video visit, you can explain what prompted you to seek an assessment and ask questions about what happens next.

It is reasonable to bring a quiz result to an appointment if it helps you explain what you have been wondering. It should not be treated as a diagnosis on its own.

### What this page can't tell you

Neither this explanation nor an online quiz can tell you whether you have ADHD. An individual assessment is where the provider reviews your information and discusses what it supports.

## 7. How do I prepare for my ADHD evaluation?

You do not need to study or arrive with a perfect file. Complete the at-home screening, choose a quiet and private place for the video visit, and jot down the questions or concerns you want to discuss. If relevant school or personal records are easy to find, you may keep them nearby.

### A simple preparation list

- Complete the screening before your appointment.
- Check your internet connection and camera, and join from a private place if possible.
- Write down what you want the provider to understand or answer.
- Gather records you already have and think may be relevant; do not delay booking to track down old paperwork.
- Have your calendar nearby if you want to note any next steps.

Your own words are enough to begin the conversation. If you are unsure whether a particular record would help, ask the practice.

### What this page can't tell you

Preparation can make it easier to use your appointment time, but it cannot determine the assessment result. You do not need to decide for yourself before you meet with the provider.
