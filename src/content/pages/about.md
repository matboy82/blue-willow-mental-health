Blue Willow Mental Health is led by a licensed psychiatric provider with a focus on ADHD. We bring computer-based screening and clinical conversation together to help adolescents and adults understand their next steps.

## Louisville roots. Kentucky reach.

The practice is based in the St. Matthews area of Louisville. Visits take place by telehealth, serving clients across Kentucky from a private space at home.

## What you can expect

- **A focused assessment:** screening results considered alongside your history.
- **Time for a conversation:** a 60–90 minute initial review.
- **Published pricing:** $225 for the initial assessment and $100 for follow-up visits.
- **A secure clinical home:** scheduling, payments, and intake through SimplePractice.

You don’t need a diagnosis before contacting us. Ask about the process, age eligibility, and whether our services fit your needs.
