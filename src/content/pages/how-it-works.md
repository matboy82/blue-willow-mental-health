## 1. Request your appointment

Start in the secure SimplePractice portal. View available times and request an initial assessment. The practice will confirm your appointment and explain the paperwork and payment steps. The initial assessment is **$225**.

You’ll need to be in Kentucky for your telehealth visit. If you’re booking for a teen, ask the practice about age eligibility and parent or guardian participation first.

## 2. Complete the screening

The practice uses computer-based ADHD screening to add objective information to the assessment. Follow the instructions provided by the practice, including any device or preparation requirements.

Screening is one part of the process. Results are considered alongside your history and a clinical interview; the screening alone does not establish a diagnosis.

## 3. Meet for a thoughtful review

Your **60–90 minute telehealth review** gives you time to discuss what has been difficult, ask questions, and go over the findings with a licensed psychiatric provider.

The conversation helps establish appropriate next steps. Ask about treatment services, follow-up care, or referral options that fit your needs.

## Before your visit

- Choose a quiet, private place with a reliable internet connection.
- Follow the practice’s instructions for screening and intake paperwork.
- Keep clinical history and documents in SimplePractice.
- Bring your questions about the process and next steps.
