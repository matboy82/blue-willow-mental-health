Blue Willow Mental Health is a telehealth ADHD practice in Louisville, Kentucky, offering flat-fee assessments for teens and adults. The initial assessment is **$225** and follow-up visits are **$100**. Visits take place by secure video across Kentucky; the practice does not offer in-person appointments.

## A focused assessment, with clear next steps

Book through the secure SimplePractice portal, complete the practice’s computer-based screening, and meet for a **60–90 minute clinical review**. The provider considers your history and screening results together. Screening alone does not establish a diagnosis.

Explore [assessments for adults](/adhd-assessment-for-adults/) or [assessments for teens and their families](/adhd-assessment-for-teens/). If you are booking for a teen, contact the practice to confirm age eligibility and guardian participation.

## How much does an ADHD assessment cost in Louisville?

At Blue Willow Mental Health, the initial assessment is **$225 flat**, and follow-ups are **$100** each. Care is cash pay. [See pricing and payment information](/pricing/).

A focused assessment differs from a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation. Ask the practice whether this service fits any school, workplace, or other documentation requirements before booking.

## When can I be seen?

Check the [secure appointment portal](https://jo-elbert.clientsecure.me/) for current openings. Your request requires confirmation from the practice. Scheduling, payments, and clinical paperwork stay in SimplePractice.

## Can I have an assessment elsewhere in Kentucky?

Yes. Blue Willow is based in Louisville’s St. Matthews area and provides telehealth across Kentucky. You need to be in Kentucky for your visit, with a private space and a reliable internet connection.
