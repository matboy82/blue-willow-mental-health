When schoolwork, routines, or everyday responsibilities are difficult, it can be hard to know what is going on. An ADHD assessment is a chance to ask questions and get a clearer understanding of the next step.

Blue Willow Mental Health works with adolescents and their families by telehealth across Kentucky. The practice is rooted in the St. Matthews area of Louisville.

## A fuller picture

Computer-based screening provides information for the clinical review. A licensed psychiatric provider considers the screening alongside history and conversation. The process does not assume ADHD is the answer.

## Before booking for your teen

Contact the practice to confirm age eligibility, consent requirements, and how a parent or guardian participates. If you need documentation for school accommodations, ask whether the practice’s assessment meets your school’s requirements.

The initial assessment is **$225**, including a **60–90 minute clinical review**. [Learn about the process](/how-it-works/) or [ask about eligibility](/contact/).
