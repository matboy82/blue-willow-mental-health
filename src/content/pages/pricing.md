Blue Willow Mental Health is a cash-pay practice. The initial assessment is **$225**, and follow-up visits are **$100** each. Payments and appointment paperwork are handled through SimplePractice.

## Before you book

Confirm the services included in your visit, payment timing, and cancellation policy with the practice. If you plan to use HSA/FSA funds, check eligibility and required documentation with your plan administrator and the practice.

A focused ADHD assessment is different from a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation. If you need testing for a specific school, workplace, or other documentation requirement, ask whether this assessment meets that need before booking.
