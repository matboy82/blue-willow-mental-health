Maybe you’ve wondered about ADHD for years. Maybe the strategies that used to get you through the day are taking more effort. You can ask for an assessment without having the answer in advance.

Blue Willow Mental Health offers focused ADHD assessments for adults by telehealth across Kentucky, rooted in Louisville’s St. Matthews area.

## Your experience matters

The process combines computer-based screening with a clinical review of your history and current concerns. Screening helps inform the conversation; it does not replace a provider’s assessment.

## Know what comes next

The **$225 initial assessment** includes a **60–90 minute review**. Talk with the practice about appropriate next steps, the services it offers, and whether follow-up care fits your needs. Follow-up visits are **$100**.

Care is cash pay. Ask about payment details, documentation, and any questions about your privacy before booking.
