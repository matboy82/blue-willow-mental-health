## The public website

This website provides information about the practice and links to scheduling and inquiries. It has no public medical intake form, symptom quiz, payment form, or patient database. Do not put medical details in website URLs.

The website uses fonts and the Psychology Today verification badge image hosted with the site. The badge links to the provider’s external profile; it does not load Psychology Today’s script on this site.

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures public page traffic and performance. We do not use advertising pixels, session recording, or marketing cookies.

For marketing attribution, registered public campaign codes (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`) are kept in your browser’s sessionStorage for the current tab session. A new tagged visit replaces them; ordinary navigation keeps them. Unknown values are discarded. When you follow a booking link, we record its public page, appointment category, link or agent handoff, and campaign codes in Cloudflare Analytics Engine. We do not save names, contact details, symptoms, full URLs, referrers, IP addresses, or patient identifiers in those event records. Hosting providers still process technical request metadata as described below.

A booking-link click is not a confirmed appointment or a payment. The campaign codes are also appended to the secure scheduling link, with `direct` used when there is no registered source. SimplePractice controls whether it uses those codes in its own records or notifications.

## Hosting

The site is designed to be hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect the website. This is not an appropriate place to send clinical information.

## SimplePractice

Scheduling, payment, clinical intake, and secure communications happen in SimplePractice. Following a portal link or loading the contact form connects you to that service. Its own terms, privacy practices, and the practice’s clinical privacy documents apply there.

The contact and booking buttons take you to SimplePractice. No third-party form or scheduling widget is embedded in this website. Booking links pass only the registered campaign codes described above; other query parameters and referrer information are not passed to the portal. Contact links do not carry campaign codes.

## Your clinical privacy rights

This website notice does not replace the practice’s Notice of Privacy Practices. Request that document and direct privacy questions to the practice through [the secure contact path](/contact/).

## Urgent needs

The website and inquiry form are not emergency services. In immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988.
