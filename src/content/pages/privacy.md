## The public website

This website provides information about the practice and links to scheduling and inquiries. It has no public medical intake form, symptom quiz, payment form, or patient database. Do not put medical details in website URLs.

The website uses fonts hosted with the site. It does not currently load an analytics script, advertising pixels, session recording, or marketing cookies.

## Hosting

The site is designed to be hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, to deliver and protect the website. This is not an appropriate place to send clinical information.

## SimplePractice

Scheduling, payment, clinical intake, and secure communications happen in SimplePractice. Following a portal link or loading the contact form connects you to that service. Its own terms, privacy practices, and the practice’s clinical privacy documents apply there.

The contact and booking buttons take you to SimplePractice. No third-party form or scheduling widget is embedded in this website. Public pages do not send URL query parameters or referrer information to the portal.

## Your clinical privacy rights

This website notice does not replace the practice’s Notice of Privacy Practices. Request that document and direct privacy questions to the practice through [the secure contact path](/contact/).

## Urgent needs

The website and inquiry form are not emergency services. In immediate danger, call 911. For crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988.
