## What the video visit is like

Before the appointment, you complete the at-home screening. During the visit, the provider reviews it with you and asks follow-up questions. You can explain what brought you in, share context, and ask questions about the process. The visit is intended to be a focused assessment, not a quick quiz or a rushed check-in.

Telehealth means you do not need to travel to an office for the appointment. Blue Willow offers assessments by telehealth across Kentucky. Use a private room if you can, and let the practice know if you need help connecting to the visit.

## What this page can't tell you

Knowing what happens on video can make the appointment feel more familiar, but it cannot predict what your assessment will find. Only a provider who reviews your information with you can address that question.
