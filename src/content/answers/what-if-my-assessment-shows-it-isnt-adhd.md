## The assessment is meant to consider your situation

The provider reviews your screening and history with you rather than assuming the answer in advance. If ADHD is not supported, you can ask what the assessment did and did not establish and what next steps may make sense. The specific guidance depends on your individual appointment.

## What this page can't tell you

This page cannot predict your result or identify another explanation for what you are experiencing. Only an assessment can address your individual situation.
