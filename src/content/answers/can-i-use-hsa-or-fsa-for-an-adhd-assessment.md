## What to check

Ask your plan administrator whether this type of assessment is an eligible expense, whether you need documentation, and whether the rules differ for your account. Blue Willow's cash-pay arrangement means you pay the practice directly; your plan administrator can explain any reimbursement process that applies to you.

## What this page can't tell you

General information cannot determine whether your specific plan will accept the expense. Your plan administrator can answer that question. The assessment itself is a separate process for discussing your individual health concerns.

## Book an assessment

The initial telehealth assessment is **$225 flat** and lasts **60–90 minutes**. Blue Willow serves clients across Kentucky. Confirm your plan's rules, then review appointment options in the [secure booking portal](https://jo-elbert.clientsecure.me/request).
