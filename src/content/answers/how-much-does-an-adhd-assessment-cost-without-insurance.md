## What the price covers

The $225 price is the listed fee for the initial assessment. Project materials describe the appointment as including an at-home screening and a 60–90 minute provider review. The listed follow-up price is $100. If you want to know whether a particular service or later appointment has a separate fee, ask the practice before booking.

Blue Willow is cash pay, so it does not bill insurance for the assessment. If you have an HSA or FSA, ask your plan administrator whether the expense qualifies under your plan. Eligibility and reimbursement rules can vary.

## What this page can't tell you

Knowing the fee can help you plan, but the price cannot tell you whether an assessment is right for your situation or what it will find. Those are questions to discuss with the practice and provider.
