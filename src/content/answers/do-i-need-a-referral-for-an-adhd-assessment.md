## How to get started

Choose an appointment through the booking portal and follow the instructions there. If you have a question about whether the service fits your needs, contact the practice before scheduling. You do not need to arrange a referral first according to the current practice materials.

## What this page can't tell you

Being able to book directly removes one step from the process; it does not establish whether ADHD is the right explanation for your concerns. That question is considered during an assessment.

## Book an assessment

The initial assessment is **$225 flat**, with a **60–90 minute** video visit. Telehealth is available across Kentucky. View current openings in the [secure Blue Willow booking portal](https://jo-elbert.clientsecure.me/request).
