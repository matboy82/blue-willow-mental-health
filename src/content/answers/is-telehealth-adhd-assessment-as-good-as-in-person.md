## What telehealth changes

You attend the appointment by video from a private location instead of traveling to an office. The assessment still includes time to review your history and screening and to ask questions. You will need a device and an internet connection that can support a video visit.

If you have questions about privacy, technology, or whether a video appointment can meet your needs, contact the practice before scheduling. The practice can explain its process and any limitations that apply to your situation.

## What this page can't tell you

This page can describe the format, but it cannot judge which format is right for you or what your assessment will find. Ask the provider about your specific needs.
