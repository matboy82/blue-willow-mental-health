## Planning the appointment as a parent or caregiver

The booking portal can show current appointment options. Before the visit, ask the practice what the teenager should complete, whether a parent or caregiver should join for any part, and whether school records or other information would be useful. Those details can depend on the practice's current process and the teen's circumstances, so confirm them directly rather than guessing.

If the school year is busy, you can still ask about the process and available appointments. You do not need to decide on a label before starting a conversation with the provider.

## What this page can't tell you

An explanation of the process cannot say whether a particular teenager has ADHD. The assessment considers that teenager's individual information and provides an opportunity to discuss next steps.
