## Check current availability

The booking portal is the best place to see appointment options as they stand now. You can review the available times before completing the booking steps. If you have a deadline or need a particular time of day, ask the practice whether it can accommodate it.

Some practices may have longer waits than others, but wait times vary. A general estimate can become outdated quickly, so confirm directly before making plans around it.

## What this page can't tell you

An availability estimate cannot tell you what an assessment will find. The assessment addresses your individual question once you meet with the provider.
