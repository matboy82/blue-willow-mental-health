## Plan for the appointment

Set aside the full appointment window and a little time beforehand to get settled. Complete the screening when you receive it, rather than trying to fit it into the video visit. If you have records or notes you want to discuss, keep them nearby, but there is no need to prepare a presentation.

A short online quiz is a different kind of activity. Blue Willow's assessment includes a provider review and conversation, so the appointment is longer by design.

## What this page can't tell you

Appointment length describes the process, not the answer. A longer visit does not guarantee a particular conclusion. The provider considers your individual information during the assessment.
