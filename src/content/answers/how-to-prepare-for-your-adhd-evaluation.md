## A simple preparation list

- Complete the screening before your appointment.
- Check your internet connection and camera, and join from a private place if possible.
- Write down what you want the provider to understand or answer.
- Gather records you already have and think may be relevant; do not delay booking to track down old paperwork.
- Have your calendar nearby if you want to note any next steps.

Your own words are enough to begin the conversation. If you are unsure whether a particular record would help, ask the practice.

## What this page can't tell you

Preparation can make it easier to use your appointment time, but it cannot determine the assessment result. You do not need to decide for yourself before you meet with the provider.
