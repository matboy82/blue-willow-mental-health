## Screening and assessment serve different purposes

A quiz is completed on its own and may give you a result to think about. An assessment adds a provider's review of the screening and your personal context. During the video visit, you can explain what prompted you to seek an assessment and ask questions about what happens next.

It is reasonable to bring a quiz result to an appointment if it helps you explain what you have been wondering. It should not be treated as a diagnosis on its own.

## What this page can't tell you

Neither this explanation nor an online quiz can tell you whether you have ADHD. An individual assessment is where the provider reviews your information and discusses what it supports.
